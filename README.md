## How Sixt's official coupon codes actually work

Sixt's official voucher codes are applied during the booking flow on sixt.com or in the Sixt app. You search with your pick-up location, rental period and vehicle category, choose a rate, and enter the code under "Voucher and Bonus Program" before completing the booking — the discount shows up in your total immediately.

The restrictions are where most people trip up. Sixt's own help center lists them plainly:

- Codes are **only valid on new bookings paid via Pay at Pick-up**. Prepaid rates are excluded.
- They can't be applied retroactively to existing or completed bookings.
- Each code has country eligibility, vehicle group and rental period conditions — check before assuming it covers your trip.
- The rental itself must be completed before the code's expiry date.

One practical detail worth knowing: a code can only be used once per booking, but you can reuse it on future rentals. And once the booking is complete, there's no way to add a code afterwards. If you're typing one in, do it before you hit confirm.

As for where codes come from, Sixt runs seasonal campaigns through its own promo pages (past examples include up to 20% off fall rentals and prepaid-only deals up to 25%), and subscribing to the Sixt newsletter has been a standing route to a 10% discount on a first rental. Third-party coupon aggregators list plenty of "codes," but many are expired or region-locked — always verify the terms on Sixt's own booking flow before counting on one.

**Is 10% cash back calculated on the full price I paid?**
No — it's calculated before taxes and fees, and it follows the discounted (member) rate you're actually charged. Budget with the pre-tax base amount.

**Does the cash back still pay if I modify or cancel the booking?**
